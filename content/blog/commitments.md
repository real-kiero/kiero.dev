# Milestones

Throughout my working career as a developer, AI has equal parts impressed me and scared me.
AI has enabled me to move considerably faster as a developer and Claude has cemented itself as a critical piece of my workplace's dev team.

Whilst I now couldn't imagine a modern dev workforce without AI, I've been continuing to question where my value lies as a developer. Following some recent personal findings. I've decided I wanted to set myself a few benchmarks to reach <u>before my 30<sup>th</sup> birthday (*17/05/2029*)</u>.

I'm writing this post to hold myself publicly accountable for completion of the **following goals**:
* Full conversational Mandarin Chinese (HSK 5 competency or higher)
* Develop senior level thinking and programming competency 
* Acquire AWS SAA-C03 Certification
* Fix sleep schedule (achieve at least 8-9 hours of sleep)

## Strategy
I've adopted various strategies to achieve these goals. I've ordered them from what I believe will be the most successful to least if followed and maintained. 

#### Technical fundamentals
I've decided on a few resources. Particularly, I'm going to commit to completing python challenges and daily programming
puzzles. [Python Morsels](https://www.pythonmorsels.com/) has been my first choice and I'm currently debating reading the weekly digests. I'm also plan to start working
through [NeetCode](https://neetcode.io/).

The goal is tighten fundamentals and maintain my reasoning skills. Being able to programmatically think and problem solve is a skill I would like to 
sharpen and develop. 

As a personal note, I struggled immensely with the TwoSum challenge. This is marked as an `Easy` on [Leetcode](https://leetcode.com/). Easy challenges are 
something I want to be able to breeze though. Whilst I understand algorithms are not indicative of a developer's skills, the embarrassment I felt over the problem as 
someone claiming to be mid level was eye-opening. 

#### Mandarin Chinese 
It's now been 5+ years married to my wife, with a beginner grasp of the language. Whilst my pronunciation is generally good, my range and vocabulary
is "survival" at best. I need to hold myself to a higher standard and apply myself. I've set myself the goal of being HSK 5 competent in communication alone. It's not an
unreasonable goal. 

For reference HSK 5 would reflect "Accurate and appropriate communication in work and academic situations". HelloChinese is regarded as one of the best
"gamified" learning platforms for beginner Chinese with proper emphasis on tones and pronunciation.

I figure this, along with a properly reviewed Anki deck for spaced repetition, should give me a leg up for meeting this goal.

#### Break from gaming
Aside from the schedule, I've almost entirely dropped gaming from my life at this point. I no longer find the same satisfaction I used to. Steam has been completely
removed from my desktop as well as Discord. Whilst Discord isn't exclusively a gaming application, I find it's easy to lose sight of my goals reading the conversation
histories.

Gaming has long been my single largest crutch so, at least for awhile I want to distance myself from this hobby dedicate more time to study and to more passive 
leisure activities like watching movies and reading.
